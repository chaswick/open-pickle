Why use it

A little more fun for the games you already play.

Bring your friends, play your games, and keep the results in one place. Your own group is enough to get started.

A session for your crew

One person starts the session and shares the invite. Everyone plays together, one player logs each result, and an opponent confirms it.

Season trophies and ratings bands

Keep track of your progress with activity achievements, season trophies, and standings against the people you play.

Room to keep playing

Mix things up with a round robin, follow your season standings, or look back at your match history. Start with what suits your group.

Who are you playing with next?

Create a free account, start a session, and send the invite to your friends. Your next game is all you need to get started.
